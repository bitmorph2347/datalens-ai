1) Pick the tournament and your team → 2) load your squad and remove anyone you are releasing → 3) find gaps → 4) get the best-value buys from players actually available (not on other teams) within your purse.

Here a higher score is better, so put a minus sign on metrics where lower is better (economy): -0.5*z(death_economy)+0.3*z(death_wickets)+0.2*z(death_dot_pct). Leave a formula box empty to use the default.
