# DataLens AI - cricket analytics

Rank players by role with your own formulas, compare matchups, profile venues, find replacements and plan an
auction squad. Covers T20 and ODI cricket, men's and women's (internationals plus about 30 leagues).

- Source code: https://github.com/bitmorph2347/datalens-ai
- Contains ball-by-ball data from [Cricsheet](https://cricsheet.org), used under the
  [Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1.0/).
- Roles, ratings and similarity are inferred from data: decision support, not scouting, betting or financial advice.
- Auction prices are synthetic examples unless you upload your own.
- Uploads and saved items are private to your browser session and are deleted when the session expires.
